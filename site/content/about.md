Kontana is a privacy-first cash tracker with a forever-free, open-source core built for people who prefer cash — for privacy, discipline, or savings.

## Why cash?

Many people find cash helps reduce impulse spending, improves awareness, and increases privacy. Kontana is designed for that lifestyle.

## What Kontana does

- **On-device cash log** — Track cash-in and cash-out with categories and notes.
- **No bank linking** — No account aggregation, no data brokerage.
- **Offline-first** — Works without internet. Cash doesn't need Wi-Fi.
- **Optional exports** — JSON and PDF, only when you choose.
- **Privacy by default** — No analytics, no tracking, no cloud sync.

## Free core. Forever. Open source.

The core is licensed under MPL-2.0 and stays free without a subscription. Wallets, payments, budgets, full local history, backups and recovery remain part of the core. Optional paid extras may follow; MCP connections and camera-assisted counting are exploratory and are not available yet. Export a JSON backup to keep a copy you control.

[View the source](https://github.com/plasticrutherfordium/getkontana) or read [how licensing and optional extras work](/open-source/).

## How it's built

- Local-first architecture: your data stays on your device.
- Open development: feedback shapes the roadmap.
- Minimal dependencies: small, fast, private.
