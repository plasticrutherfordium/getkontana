## The core stays free. Forever.

Wallets, payments, budgets, your complete local history, JSON backups/import and PDF reports are part of the free core. No account or subscription is required. You can keep using the core without buying an extra.

## Optional paid extras

We may offer optional paid extras, such as MCP connections to AI assistants, managed AI services and camera-assisted counting of notes and coins. **These are exploratory features and are not available yet.** There is no active Premium plan or checkout.

The core remains useful on its own. History, backups, recovery and manual cash tracking stay free. Any separate paid component will identify its own licence and terms; the core remains MPL-2.0.

## Privacy stays a choice

The current core stores your cash data on your device. Future connections or cloud AI would require an explicit opt-in and clear information about which data or images leave your device. Connecting a local MCP server to a cloud AI assistant can still share selected data with that assistant's provider. Camera counting would propose results for you to review before saving them.
